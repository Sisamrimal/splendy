Privacy Policy

Your finances are personal. Here's exactly what we collect, why we collect it, and how we keep it safe.

Last updated · November 26, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Spendly ("we", "us") collects, uses, and protects your information when you use our personal expense tracking service ("Service"). By using Spendly, you agree to the practices described below.

01Data We Collect

We only collect what we need to run the Service:

  • Account information — your name, email address, and a securely hashed password.
  • Expense data — the amounts, categories, dates, and descriptions you choose to enter.
  • Basic usage data — information such as login times and browser type, used to keep the Service reliable and secure.

We do not collect bank account numbers, card details, or access to your financial institutions.

02How We Use Your Data

Your information is used solely to provide and improve Spendly, including to:

  • Create and manage your account.
  • Store, display, and summarize your expenses.
  • Generate category breakdowns and spending insights for you.
  • Protect the Service against fraud, abuse, and unauthorized access.

We never sell your personal or financial data, and we do not use it for advertising.

03Data Storage

Your data is stored on secured servers, and passwords are stored only in hashed form. We take reasonable technical and organizational measures to protect your information, though no system can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

We retain your data for as long as your account is active. If you delete your account, your personal and expense data will be permanently removed from our systems within a reasonable period.

04Third Party Services

We may rely on trusted third-party providers for services such as hosting and font delivery. These providers only receive the minimum information necessary to perform their function and are expected to protect it appropriately.

We do not share your expense data with third parties for their own marketing purposes. We may disclose information if required by law.

05Contact Us

If you have questions about this Privacy Policy, or would like to access, correct, or delete your data, please reach out to us at privacy@spendly.example.

You can also review our Terms and Conditions for more information about using the Service.
